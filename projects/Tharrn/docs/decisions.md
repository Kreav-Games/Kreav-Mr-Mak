## Open questions

| ID | Question | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| OQ-01 | Consoles ("60 FPS consoles", direction §6.2) | PC first. Console builds need platform developer accounts, dev kits and their SDKs; decide when the PC prototype is fun |
| OQ-02 | Dedicated servers | Develop with PIE "Play as Client" (a server inside the editor). Packaging a dedicated server needs UE 5.8.3 built from source (GitHub access through the owner's Epic account, about 200 GB and several hours to build); do it before the vertical slice's co-op extraction |
| OQ-03 | MetaHuman character creation with 120 face sliders at runtime (direction §1.3) | Checked in the installed engine (plugin descriptors, 2026-10-15): MetaHuman Creator's face technology (`MetaHumanCoreTechLib`) and the character editor are **editor-only** in 5.8.3, so they cannot ship in the game. What can run in the game: assembled MetaHumans and their palettes (`MetaHumanCharacter`, Beta), crowds (`MetaHumanCrowd`, Experimental), **Mutable** (Beta; in-game customisation with morphs baked in the editor, which [Epic's guide](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/using-mutable-and-metahumans-in-unreal-engine) says cannot do MetaHuman's face blending) and **DNACalib** (a runtime library that edits a face rig's DNA). Recommendation: prototype with the 12 presets plus body scale, materials, tattoos and implants (P8); then a research module for sliders, either Mutable morph targets or blending between preset DNAs with DNACalib, judged on whether facial animation still holds |
| OQ-04 | Missing art: creatures (Skitterjaw first) (weapons, clothing and armour: D-22; weapon animations: MSS, D-23) | The owner picks Fab packs or has them made; Claude checks skeleton fit and cost before staging |
| OQ-05 | Ocean and coast for the archipelago | Test the Water plugin's ocean early in the Coastal slice (it failed on rivers before, and is still Experimental in 5.8.3, checked 2026-10-15); fallback: a custom ocean plane with a single-layer water material |
| OQ-06 | Terrain for 16–25 km² | Real coastal or island lidar through `Tools/Terrain` for the starter area; generated terrain where no real landform fits; decide with the Coastal slice |
| OQ-07 | Scope | The direction is a studio roadmap of 18 months or more (raids, shards, live ops). Build its Prototype first (core gunfeel, vitals, one biome, 3 creatures, MetaHuman base) and re-plan after it |
| ~~OQ-08~~ | ~~First- or third-person camera~~ | Resolved by D-15 |
| OQ-09 | Hit detection and lag compensation | Hitscan validated on the server with rewind of hit boxes, projectiles simulated on the server with client-side prediction; prove with 2 clients and simulated latency in the combat module. **Built in P3 (2026-10-16):** that, plus the shooter's view taken within 15 cm; 0 to 1.2 % of shots disagreed at 100 ms round trip and 1 % loss. Closes with the owner's P3 play-test |

| OQ-11 | Day length and time of day | Not specified; propose with the Coastal slice |

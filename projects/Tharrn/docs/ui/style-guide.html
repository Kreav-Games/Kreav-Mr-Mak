Interface style guide

Draft 1 for the owner's direction (D-31): The Division 2's menus and HUD, ARK's layout for the survival screens. Calm dark glass over the world, one signal colour, condensed capitals, numbers you read at a glance, and nothing on screen that the game does not know.

01The world first

Thin type and dark glass over the world, no heavy frames. The HUD sits in the corners and fades when it has nothing to say.

02One signal

Exodus orange marks what is yours and what has focus. Every other colour means one thing: health, cold, danger, safe.

03Survival at a glance

ARK's habit: hunger, hydration and temperature beside health, with values; warnings before damage.

04Honest data

Every number comes from the server's state. A feature not built yet is not drawn as if it were.

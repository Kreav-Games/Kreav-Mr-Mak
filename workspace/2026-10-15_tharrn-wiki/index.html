Tharrn

A sci-fi action-RPG survival shooter on a vast alien archipelago, for one player or many. The guide to the game as designed, and as built so far.

Where it stands, 2026-10-15: design stage. GDD 0.1 is written and the first build module, the foundation (data tables, tests, the multiplayer test harness and a grey test range), is built and checked. Nothing is playable as a game yet. Everything in this wiki is the design unless it carries the In the build label.

The premise

You are an operative of the Exodus Initiative. Your convoy was investigating an alien signal when it crashed on a vast alien archipelago, scattering the survivors and corrupting their tech. Survive, secure an extraction beacon and find out what the signal is.

Tharrn is a sci-fi action-RPG survival shooter for one player or many: precise shooting, survival that changes how you fight, sixty levels of RPG progression with dungeons and quests, and player-versus-player danger only where you choose to go.

Five pillars

Elite gunfeel

Recoil curves, weak points, stagger and movement tech that feel precise, on a model the server checks.

High-stakes survival

Vitals that change how you fight, resources worth risking a PvP zone for, and a base to hold.

Deep RPG

Sixty levels, three talent trees, quests, dungeons, blueprints and loot tiers.

Consensual danger

PvP only where you choose to go: flagged zones with richer loot.

A world that grows

Biomes, hubs and endgame regions added later as map extensions.

A session

Prepare at a hub→Expedition into a risk zone→Dungeon or raid→Extract→Return and upgrade

Daily missions; weekly world events such as apex migrations and rotating PvP hotspots; seasonal content. You earn XP, talent points, weapon mastery, blueprints and faction reputation.

The first 40 minutes

BeatWhat it teaches
Crash siteMoving, sprinting, crouching, mantling, basic aim, recoil
VitalsThe vitals appear on the HUD, and what they do (aim sway when dehydrated)
Scavenge and craftPicking up organic and mineral resources; crafting a bandage and basic ammo
First fightA creature pack: aiming at weak points, staggering. Open question The direction names the Skitterjaw, which is a desert creature, while the crash site is on the coast (OQ-12)
ShelterPlacing a foundation, deploying a generator, crafting a bench
BeaconA short mission next to a flagged PvP zone, with a warning overlay
Co-op (optional)An NPC companion or a friend joins to show team play

It ends when you have survived a 10 to 15 minute expedition, crafted the essentials and returned to a hub.

Endgame

Instanced high-risk raids, faction wars, territory control and seasonal events. New biomes arrive as map extensions reached by teleport gates or long-range vehicles. The world is persistent: killing a global boss unlocks tech for the whole server.

Glossary

TermMeaning
Exodus InitiativeThe organisation the player works for
HubA safe town with vendors, services and a mission board
FlaggedOpen to attack by other flagged players; you are flagged inside a PvP zone and for a while after leaving
Consent bufferA short delay before the flag applies on entering a PvP zone (10 s where a zone has one)
Exit cooldownHow long you must stay outside a PvP zone before the flag clears (60 s)
Amber bufferA warning belt around a PvP zone
ExtractionLeaving a dangerous area with your loot at an extraction point; others can interrupt it
Neural stabilityA vital; when it runs low you see hallucinations and your aim jitters
StaggerA meter on each enemy; filling it interrupts the enemy's attack
Weak pointA part of a creature that takes extra damage

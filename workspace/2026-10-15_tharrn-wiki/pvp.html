PvP and travel

Danger by consent: how flagging works, what you can lose, and how you get around.

Flagging

Designed Player-versus-player only happens in flagged zones. The server flags you; nothing on your side can change it.

  1. You cross into a PvP zone. A red icon and a timer appear. Where a zone has a consent buffer you get 10 seconds first.
  2. You are flagged. Other flagged players can damage you and you them. Players who are not flagged cannot be attacked.
  3. You leave. The flag stays until you have been outside for 60 seconds.

The flagged zones today: Skitterjaw Hollow, Crystal Expanse, Echo Caverns, Floating Archipelago A, Contested Ruins. Each zone sets its own consent buffer and exit cooldown, so casual and hardcore zones can differ.

What is at stake

LootFull loot on death in flagged zones (default). Secured base storage is safe unless raided
BountiesKills build a bounty; bounty hunters can track flagged players with devices
ExtractionExtraction points take time to complete, shown as a progress bar; other players can interrupt

Vehicles run on fuel you craft or find, take damage and need repair. The Sci-Fi Complete Bundle's hoverbike is the starting point, rebuilt to work online. Vehicles arrive in the alpha phase.

Travel targets across the map: about 45 minutes on foot and 15 to 20 minutes by hoverbike. At about 13 km across, that means about 5 m/s on foot and 11 to 15 m/s on the hoverbike. The character's run speed is already 5 m/s (walk 2, sprint 7), so a straight run across takes about 45 minutes and a real route with detours about an hour. 45 minutes is the straight-line target (D-19).

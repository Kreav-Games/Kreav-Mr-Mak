Survival and building

Vitals that change how you fight, resources worth the risk, and a base to hold.

Vitals

Designed Five vitals change how you fight, not only whether you live.

VitalRangeWhat it does
Hunger0–100Below 30, stamina recovers 40 % slower
Hydration0–100Below 30, aim sway grows by 25 %
TemperatureComfort range set by your armourOutside it you take damage over time and move slower
RadiationBuilds upPast thresholds it lowers your stats for a long time; needs detox
Neural stability0–100Low values bring hallucinations (visual noise, phantom enemies) and aim jitter

The formulas

StaminaRegen = BaseRegen × (1 − HungerPenalty) × (1 − TempPenalty)
AimSway      = BaseSway × (1 + (30 − Hydration)/100 × 0.8 + (100 − Neural)/100 × 0.5)

Resources

Organic→Mineral→Alloy→Biotech core→Energy crystal

Resource nodes grow back on timers (each zone has its own, see World and zones). Rare nodes appear in flagged PvP zones.

Base building

PartHow it works
ModulesFoundation, wall, door, turret, generator, filtration, crafting bench
PowerGenerators make energy, devices use it; too many devices on one network overloads it and it shuts down
DecayStructures nobody maintains decay, by default 1 % a day
ClaimsPlots in PvP zones can be claimed, with timers and upkeep costs

The building pieces come from the Sci-Fi Complete Bundle's modular outpost kit. Placing shows a ghost preview that snaps to other pieces; the server checks every placement.

Inventory and crafting

  • A grid inventory with a weight limit, filters by type and quick-craft recipes.
  • You ask to craft; the server checks the recipe and your inventory, then changes both. Crafts wait in a queue.

The first milestone

Hunger, hydration and temperature working online and driving the two formulas (with tests at the thresholds); crafting a bandage and ammo; a foundation, a generator and a bench that two players can place and both see.

Direction

  • The Division 2's menus and HUD:** calm dark glass over the world, one signal colour, condensed capitals, numbers you read at a glance; the HUD in the corners, tilted a little as if it floats beside the player, fading when it has nothing to say; full-screen menus with tabs across the top (Q and E) and a card for the selected thing.
  • ARK's layout for the survival parts:** the inventory with the backpack left, the character and what is worn in the middle with the survival stats, the other side (an item, a container, a crafting station) right; hunger, hydration and temperature beside health with their values.
  • Honest data:** every number comes from the game's real state and data; a feature not built yet is labelled, not drawn as if it were.
  • Keyboard and mouse first (D-42; the owner, 2026-10-20: "Its a pc game so using a keyboard mainly, game controller is an extra"):** every screen, prompt and control is designed and tested with keyboard and mouse; the gamepad gets working equivalents (Common UI's navigation, its buttons on the prompts while one is in use) but never shapes a layout.
  • e style is Tharrn's own (Exodus orange, Barlow type, its own icons), not copied art; Barlow is under the SIL Open Font License, so it can ship with the game.
  • Approved (D-36, 2026-10-19):** the style guide as drafted; the HUD's corners lean a little; Easy Game UI stays underneath (options, saves, input prompts, pause and menus) and is restyled in this look, while Tharrn's own C++ HUD and inventory stay.
  • The main menu's scene (the owner, 2026-10-19: "The planet should look or resemble our game assets/map, the moons should look like the asset moon we have"):** Tharrn's planet is painted from the main map itself (Tools/UI/make_planet_textures.py in the game repo reads the Map chat's terrain data: the ten regions in their colours from orbit, shaded by the real heights, the rivers and lakes, the shallow coast; the Lattice in the mask's red, strongest under the desert and the plateau) for Tharrn's own planet material (M_TharrnPlanet: the map lit by a sun direction, drifting clouds, the Lattice glowing on the night side, the atmosphere's rim, on a smooth sphere of its own; the bundle's planet material samples virtual textures, which a scene capture leaves blurred, and its mesh shows corners this big), and the sky holds the bundle's moon and two planets as the game's sky does (BP_SpaceMoonA, BP_SpacePlanetA, BP_SpacePlanetB), all in one actor, BP_TharrnMenuScene. The menu itself is WBP_TharrnMainMenu, built from the shared parts by Tools/UI/build_main_menu.py; its entries are honest to the build (no saves yet, so Deploy starts the game; character creation and a server list show disabled).
  • The logo is the owner's T emblem** (concept 2026-10-19, docs/ui/Title_MainMenu_Concept.png): carved rock with a glowing crack, over a wide metal wordmark and SURVIVAL. Rebuilt for Tharrn (draft 2, a proposal): the T cut out as it is (docs/ui/art/tharrn-emblem.png, art/cut_emblem.py); the wordmark redrawn crisp; behind it Tharrn from orbit, the carrier Lantern's view (story.md): the ocean world's night side, the great island's rock glowing along the same kind of crack as the T (the Lattice and its signal), the two planets in its sky. In the game that scene is live (SkyAtmosphere from orbit), the crack pulses with the signal and the camera drifts; the mock-ups' backdrops are drawn by art/render_orbit.py as stand-ins. Sources: docs/ui/ holds the style guide's tokens and parts (tokens.css, icons.js), the mock-ups (*.html, drawn over in-game captures in docs/ui/backdrops/) and render.mjs, which turns them into pictures in pictures/ui/ (node projects/Tharrn/docs/ui/render.mjs).

Screens to come

  • Map: layers for PvP zones, resource nodes and dungeon entrances; several zoom levels.
  • Journal: objectives, stages, lore entries and map markers.
  • Starting location: pick where to start, one or two places in each region by difficulty (D-28).
  • Accessibility: UI scale, colour-blind modes, subtitle sizes, controller remapping; offered at first launch.

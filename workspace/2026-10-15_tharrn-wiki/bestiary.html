The creatures designed so far.

Designed The direction names six creatures; three have full stats so far. No creature has art or animation yet: they need concept art, meshes with four levels of detail, hit-box maps, sounds, effects, behaviour and animation sets (idle, walk, run, attacks, stagger, death, a special move).

How they will be built

  • Behaviour runs on the server only (Unreal's StateTree, with perception); your game receives their movement and animations.
  • Every bone has a hit box; weak points are tagged hit boxes with their own damage multiplier.
  • The first three to build are the Skitterjaw, the Phase Warden and the Grav Raptor: a pack, a teleporter and a flyer, three different problems.

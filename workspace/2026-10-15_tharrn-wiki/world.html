World and zones

About 75 km² of alien archipelago in six biomes, laid out as 15 zones.

Designed The world is about 75.3 km²: 15 zones of 2.24 × 2.24 km, within a box of 13.4 × 9.0 km. It streams in as you travel. Crossing it should take about 45 minutes on foot and 15 to 20 minutes by hoverbike (targets for tuning speeds).

Six biomes

You start on the coast and push inland and upward, toward the floating islands of the endgame.

Coastal→Fungal Forest→Crystalline Desert→High-Gravity Plateau→Subterranean Caverns→Floating Archipelagos

The world will be built from the Sci-Fi Complete Bundle's jungle, dry, volcanic, arctic and moon kits. The caves cannot be ordinary terrain (it has no overhangs), and the floating islands will be their own streamed levels.

Progression and dungeons

Sixty levels, three talent trees, five loot tiers, and dungeons built from rooms.

Levels and talents

Designed Sixty levels. The XP for each next level grows by 12 %:

XP(level) = baseXP × 1.12^(level − 1)

Open question baseXP is not set yet; it will be chosen from how long reaching level 60 should take.

Each level gives one talent point for one of three trees. Nodes have prerequisites and unlock new mechanics.

TreeExample talents
CombatPrecision Training (less recoil) · Quick Reload (15 % faster) · Suppressive Fire (suppresses an area)
SurvivalEfficient Forager (+20 % resources) · Advanced Filtration (uses less water) · Field Medic (faster healing)
TechDrone Control (deploy a drone) · Power Optimization (generators use less) · Alien Synthesis (craft alien cores)

Loot tiers

Common→Uncommon→Rare→Advanced→Alien

Higher tiers need blueprints. Each zone has a loot tier (see World and zones).

Quests

Five kinds: main, side, faction, timed and dungeon quests, each in stages with objectives and rewards. The first faction questline comes with the vertical slice.

Dungeons

Instanced and assembled from room templates, with a seed and modifiers that change each run.

RoomWhat happens
Puzzle roomNeeds a crafted item or a trigger in the room
Combat arenaWaves of enemies, with cover and height
Traversal chamberPlatforming and shifting gravity
Trap corridorTimed hazards and detection
Boss arenaA boss in several phases, using the room against you

Modifiers: gravity shift (low or high), fog of war, enemy loadout variation, a time limit. Bosses change at 75, 50 and 25 % health, adding a shield, minions or a hazard, and drop unique blueprints and rare loot. Open question The direction counts six room types but names five.

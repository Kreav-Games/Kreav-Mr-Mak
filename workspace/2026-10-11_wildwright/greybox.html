Reading the greybox maps

Two placeholder maps exist in the Unreal project. They are labs built before most of the systems they will test. This page explains what each block is for and how testing works as casting comes online.

What works today (action 5 · owner play-test passed)

Water wets patches, fire burns dry fuel, and water cools hot surfaces with a Steam message.

1–4 or the mouse wheel picks Fire, Water, Earth or Air. Left mouse applies it. The crosshair shows a plus when the cast would work and an X when it would not, with the reason underneath. A second line shows moisture, temperature, fuel and stages. Materials keep the result visible. Air dispersal, soil growth and earth barriers still report “not implemented yet”.

Test range (L_TestRange): one thing per station, side by side, so each rule can be checked in isolation in seconds.
Valley (L_Valley_Greybox): the same things in context, where the first objective will be played.

How one test runs

Example: station 2, GDD test A.2 #1
  1. Walk to station 2The brown patch with the sign "wet it first, then fire".
  2. Read the signIt names the rule and says what should happen.
  3. Cast waterThe patch darkens. The HUD shows moisture 60 and Wet.
  4. Cast fireFire is refused, with a reason on screen: too wet to ignite.
  5. Compare with station 1The dry patch next to it does ignite. Same rule, different state.
PassThe result matches the sign, every time you repeat it.
FailA different result, a different result on a repeat, or a refusal without a reason.
